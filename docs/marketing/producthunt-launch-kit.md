## Listing

- **Name:** Shelfmark
- **Tagline (≤60):** Book series in order — track your reading, no account ever
- **Links:** https://shelfmark.zalize.com
- **Topics:** Books, Productivity, Privacy, Web App
- **Pricing status:** Free (open beta; planned paid plans shown at /pricing)

**Description (≤260):**
> Shelfmark lists the correct reading order for tens of thousands of book series, and lets you tick books off as you read. Progress lives in your browser — no signup, no cookies, no social feed. Plus a Year in Books report, reading goals, and an open JSON API.

## Maker comment (first comment)

> Hi PH! 👋 Shelfmark started from two tiny frustrations every series reader knows: "what order do I read these in?" and "which one was I on again?" — without wanting yet another account or social feed.
>
> So it's two things: a reading-order reference (tens of thousands of series, built from Wikidata + Open Library open data, publication order documented) and a tracker that lives entirely in your browser's localStorage. We literally cannot see what you read.
>
> Everything is free while we're in open beta. Things I'd love feedback on: the Year in Books report, the no-signup model (does it feel trustworthy?), and what data you'd want from the open API (every reading order is JSON, no key).

## FAQ answers (for comments)

- **How do you make money?** Beta is free; planned Plus plan ($2.99/mo) is published at /pricing. No payments are collected today.
- **What if I clear my browser?** Export/Import JSON backup on My Shelf; progress is yours, portable.
- **Where's the data from?** Wikidata (CC0) + Open Library, cross-checked; report errors from any series page.
- **Mobile app?** PWA — Add to Home Screen; installable with shortcuts.
- **API?** /api/series/{slug}.json and /api/authors/{slug}.json, CORS-enabled, no key.
