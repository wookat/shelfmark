    Looking to switch?

    Import from Goodreads

    There's no need to start over -- our automated import tool can bring your existing Goodreads library with you. No fuss.

    Data & Privacy

    Retain control of your data

    Your data is your business, not ours. We'll never sell it to somebody else and we don't use invasive tracking that advertisers rely on. And since you're in charge, you're free to export your data and take it with you.

    Is this sustainable?

    We are member supported

    We choose to charge our members directly for extra benefits so that we profit when we make them happy instead of when we show them ads.

    Meet the team

    As three friends scattered throughout Europe, we were looking for a way to discover books and share our own recommendations. Nothing cut the mustard, so we took the challenge on. We may not have deep pockets or lots of time, but we're willing to bet we can make up for it by working smart and taking pride in our work.

    Sofia, Bulgaria
